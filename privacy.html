Redwood Agent Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Who this applies to

Redwood Agent is a private, personal app operated by its owner for their own household. It is not offered to the public. The only Google account it is used with is the owner's.

What data it accesses

Only data the account owner explicitly authorizes through Google's consent screen. Currently this is the Google Smart Device Management API (sdm.service scope) for devices the owner selects: device names and structure, thermostat mode, temperature setpoints, ambient temperature, humidity, heating/cooling status, and related device events. If other Google APIs are added later, they are accessed only after the owner grants those permissions on Google's consent screen.

How the data is used

To record and analyze the owner's home heating and cooling performance and energy efficiency. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the owner's household.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on a private server controlled by the owner. It is not sold, rented, or transferred to third parties, and no people other than the owner have access to it.

Redwood Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions or, for Nest devices, from Nest partner connections.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
